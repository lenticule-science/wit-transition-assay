# WIT transition assay settlement law

whether two immutable RMN effect cells, bound to one evidence identity, form one
and only one cover transition in FOUR's knowledge order.

create evidence, price the transition, or keep an ambient book. One canonical
typed RMN request crosses its signed A2A face; one provider-bound receipt and
one append-only RWIL/RDF record leave it.
